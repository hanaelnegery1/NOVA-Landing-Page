Why choose NOVA

Premium Quality

Full-grain leather, brushed steel and durable acetate. Materials chosen to age well, not wear out.

Carefully Selected Products

We keep the range small on purpose. Every item is tested and approved before it joins the collection.

Simple & Timeless Style

Quiet colours and clean shapes that work with what you already own, season after season.

Quality, simplicity and everyday style

NOVA started with a simple question: why is it so hard to find everyday things that are both well made and easy to wear?

We answer it by choosing less. Each bag, watch, pair of sunglasses and accessory has to earn its place through good materials, honest design and real daily use.

The result is a collection that feels calm, looks refined and never tries too hard.
